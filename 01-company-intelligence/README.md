# Cloudflare — Company Intelligence Investigation

## Objective

This investigation analyzes Cloudflare, Inc. using publicly
available information.

## Investigation Areas

- Company identity
- Founders and key people
- Products and services
- Corporate structure
- Domains and digital presence
- Competitors
- News and significant events
- Official social presence

## Target

Cloudflare, Inc.

Official Website: https://www.cloudflare.com/

## Methodology

The investigation uses publicly available sources and
cross-verification where possible.

## Files

- `report.md` — Full investigation report
- `report.pdf` — PDF version of the report
- `sources.md` — Sources used during the investigation
- `screenshots/` — Supporting screenshots
- `evidence/` — Additional evidence

## Disclaimer

This investigation is based on publicly available information.
No private, leaked, or restricted information was intentionally used.
